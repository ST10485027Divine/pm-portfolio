# Week 1: Project Charter

## Project name
HeyClicky Student Plan Launch, South Africa

## Background
HeyClicky is a young startup, founded in 2026 and based in San Francisco. Its app gives people a very simple way to talk to AI models and to set AI helpers to work on tasks. Students are a natural audience, since they use AI for studying, writing and research, but they usually have little money to spend on subscriptions.

## Purpose
This project launches a discounted plan for students in South Africa. Right now a student here pays full price in dollars, which is expensive when the rand is weak. A student plan priced for local students, backed by a campaign on campuses and social media, would grow HeyClicky's user base in a new market and build loyalty early.

## Objectives
1. Launch student pricing and a local campaign within 4 months of the charter being approved.
2. Reach 1,000 student sign-ups within 2 months of launch.
3. Keep total launch spending within R250,000 (an invented figure for practice).

## Scope
**In scope:**
- A student plan with rand pricing
- A way to check that someone is really a student
- A South African launch campaign (social media, student societies and campus ambassadors)
- A simple way to measure sign-ups

**Out of scope:**
- New product features
- Launching in other countries
- Changes to non-student pricing
- Partnerships with universities that need formal contracts

## Deliverables
- Agreed student pricing in rand
- Working student verification and sign-up flow
- Launch campaign materials
- A sign-up tracking sheet
- A short launch report

## Key milestones
| Milestone | Target month |
|---|---|
| Charter approved | Month 0 |
| Pricing and verification method agreed | Month 1 |
| Student sign-up and verification built and tested | Month 2 |
| Campaign ready and ambassadors recruited | Month 3 |
| Launch | Month 4 |
| 1,000 sign-ups reached | Month 6 |

## Stakeholders
| Who | Role | What they care about |
|---|---|---|
| HeyClicky founder (pretend) | Sponsor | Growth in a new market, within budget |
| Dee | Project manager | Delivering on time and on budget |
| Developer | Builds sign-up and verification | A clear, realistic list of requirements |
| Marketing lead | Runs the campaign | A budget and a launch date they can plan around |
| Campus ambassadors and student societies | Spread the word | Something worth sharing, and a fair reward |
| Payment provider | Handles rand payments | Correct setup and low fraud |
| Privacy adviser | Checks personal data handling | Following POPIA, South Africa's law on protecting personal information |
| Students | Customers | A fair price, and sign-up that is quick and simple |

## Budget
Rough ceiling: R250,000 (invented). The detailed budget comes in Week 2.

## Assumptions and constraints
- The small team can build student verification in about 8 weeks.
- Students can be verified using a university email address or a student card.
- The team is very small, so people may be working on more than one thing.
- The launch date should avoid exam periods, when students have little time to try new tools.

## Top 3 risks
1. **Verification takes longer than planned,** which delays the launch.
2. **Low awareness among students,** so sign-ups stay below 1,000.
3. **Price still too high for students,** so people look but don't subscribe.
