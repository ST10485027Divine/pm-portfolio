# pm-portfolio

> Eight projects, one running case: from a launch plan and budget to risk logs, roadmaps and status reports. Built to prove I can deliver projects, not just talk about it.

## Hi, I'm Divine Zanao
I'm a Computer and Information science  student in Cape Town, graduating in 2028, and I'm aiming to become a project manager. I'm not great at coding, but I love how things get planned, tracked and delivered. This repo is where I show it, one project at a time.

## The idea
I took the skills that real graduate job posts ask for and turned each one into a small project. Every week I make something you can open and read: a plan, a budget, a risk log, a report.

**The running case:** a pretend student plan launch for HeyClicky in South Africa. It's an independent practice project and isn't connected to the company. Everything here uses public information, and the numbers are invented.
