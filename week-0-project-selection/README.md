# Week 0: Project Selection

## Purpose
Before planning anything, I compared several possible projects and chose one on purpose. Choosing well is the first step of any project.

## Candidates and objectives
| Project | Objective (invented numbers, for practice) |
|---|---|
| HeyClicky student plan (South Africa) | Launch student pricing and a local campaign in 4 months, reaching 1,000 student sign-ups in the first 2 months |
| Takealot pickup points | Open 20 pickup points in Cape Town in 5 months, with 15% of Cape Town orders choosing pickup in the first 3 months |
| Canva student templates | Launch 20 templates in 3 months, reaching 50,000 downloads in the first quarter |
| Spotify study playlists | Launch in 4 months, with 10% of student users trying it in the first month |

## Scoring (1 to 5, higher is better)
| Project | Skills shown | Realistic plan | Public info | Enjoyment | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HeyClicky | 3 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 15 |
| Spotify | 4 | 2 | 4 | 5 | 15 |
| Takealot | 5 | 4 | 4 | 1| 14 |
| Canva | 4 | 3 | 4 | 5| 16 |

- **Skills shown:** how many project, product and program skills it lets me practise
- **Realistic plan:** how believable a plan for it would be
- **Public info:** how much I can find without inside knowledge
- **Enjoyment:** how much I'd enjoy planning it

## Decision
I chose HeyClicky because it was a project that i would realistically enjoy, and its a new organisation with a small team.

## Assumptions and limits
- This is an independent practice project and is not connected to any of these companies.
- I used only public information.
- All numbers are invented for practice.
